Distances are centre to centre, geometric. to perigee: how near to perigee the Moon is,
0% at apogee and 100% at perigee. A new or full Moon at least 90 % of the way from
apogee to perigee: (apogee - d) / (apogee - perigee) >= 0.9, with the perigee and the
apogee on either side of it in time (Nolle 1979). A new or full Moon at least 90 % of
the way to apogee: the same fraction <= 0.1.
