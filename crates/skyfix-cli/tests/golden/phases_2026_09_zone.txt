The instants at which the Moon's apparent geocentric ecliptic longitude minus the Sun's
is 0, 90, 180 and 270 degrees (CONVENTIONS 13.5), to the nearest second; --format json
carries the milliseconds. The local column is the date and time in UTC-04:00; the
instants themselves are the same everywhere on Earth, and --format json keeps them in
UTC. A date given to --from or --to is a date in that zone, from local midnight.
