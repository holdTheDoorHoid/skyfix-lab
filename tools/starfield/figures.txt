# Constellation figures for the SkyFix Lab star field. Display only.
#
# This project's own drawing, covered by the project's MIT OR Apache-2.0 licence. The
# figures follow the traditional modern Western stick figures, drawn for this project
# from the shape of each constellation and the catalogue positions of its stars. No
# existing figure dataset (Stellarium, d3-celestial, the IAU / Sky & Telescope charts or
# any other) was copied or consulted.
#
# Format: "<IAU abbreviation>: star - star - star ...", one chain per line; a
# constellation may have several chains. A star is a designation as in names.txt (Bayer
# letter with optional superscript, or Flamsteed number, plus constellation) or
# "HR <n>" for stars the catalogue gives no Bayer or Flamsteed designation (the Latin-
# letter stars of Carina, Puppis and Vela, for example). tools/starfield/build.py turns
# every chain into star pairs by HR number and checks that each star exists, lies in or
# beside its constellation, and that no segment is absurdly long.
